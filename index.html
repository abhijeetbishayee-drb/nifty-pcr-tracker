Sensex

SENSEX is a BSE index and its weeklies expire on Thursday. The band is set to the same share of spot as Nifty's rather than the same number of points — 300 and 1000, against Nifty's 100 and 500, because Sensex trades around 3.2× its level. There is no history line below: the backfill reads NSE's F&O bhavcopy, which does not carry a BSE index.
PCR = Put OI ÷ Call OI over a near-ATM strike band (weekly: 100pt steps, monthly: 500pt steps). Bank Nifty shows monthly only — NSE discontinued its weekly expiry in 2023. Violation: PCR < 0.7 or > 2.0 — price tends to gravitate toward the equilibrium strike (near-equal Call/Put OI). 0.41→0.80 crossing is an intraday reversal/SL signal — give it 15-30 min to stabilize before acting.
